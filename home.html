GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your first shift.

What is a restaurant menu matrix?+

A menu matrix brings a dish's ingredients, allergen notes, description, price, and service details into a consistent reference. Menu Matrix makes that reference searchable and adds photos, flashcards, and quizzes for staff training.

Can I try Menu Matrix without an account?+

Yes. Open the demo menu to explore sample items, ingredient and allergen filters, flashcards, and quizzes. You can also view a shared menu when its creator gives you a menu code.

Do the website and Android app use the same menus?+

Yes. They connect to the same Firebase backend. With an internet connection and a current app version, menu changes sync between devices.

Can I control who edits a menu?+

Yes. Menu Matrix supports roles and menu access permissions. Authorized managers can give editors access to selected categories while other team members use the menu to view and study.

Can I use my existing menu PDF?+

Yes. Use the PDF import option to bring menu information into the app. Review the extracted items, prices, ingredients, and allergens before using them for service or training.

Is Menu Matrix an ordering system?+

Menu Matrix is designed for restaurant menu knowledge and staff training. Its focus is helping teams learn what they serve through a clear menu reference and practice tools.
